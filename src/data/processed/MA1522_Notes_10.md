# Transpose:
$$A_{m \times n} \to A_{n \times m}$$
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \\ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix}_{m \times n} \to A^T = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{21} & \dots & a_{m1} \\ a_{12} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{m2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{1n} & a_{2n} & \dots & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix}_{n \times m}$$

$$(A^T)^T = A$$
$$(cA)^T = cA^T$$
$$(A + B)^T = A^T + B^T$$
$$(AB)^T = B^T A^T \to (A_1 A_2 \dots A_k)^T = A_k^T \dots A_2^T A_1^T$$

$A$ is symmetrical if and only if $A^T = A$
