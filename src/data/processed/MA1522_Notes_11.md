Vector equation:
$$x_1 \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \\ a_{21} \\ \dots \\ a_{m1} \end{pmatrix} + x_2 \begin{pmatrix} a_{12} \\ a_{22} \\ \dots \\ a_{m2} \end{pmatrix} + \dots + x_n \begin{pmatrix} a_{1n} \\ a_{2n} \\ \dots \\ a_{mn} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} b_1 \\ b_2 \\ \dots \\ b_m \end{pmatrix}$$

$$m \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix}$$

Properties of homogenous linear system:

$$A_{mxn} x = 0_{mx1} \quad \text{homogenous system ALWAYS consistent}$$

* zero solution is called trivial solution
* $x \neq 0 \to$ solution non-trivial solution
* Homogenous system has $\infty$ solutions if and only if it has non-trivial solution.
