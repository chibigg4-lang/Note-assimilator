Theorem
Every elementary matrices $\mathbf{E}$ are invertible. The inverse $\mathbf{E}^{-1}$ is the elementary matrix corresponding to the reverse of the row operation corresponding to $\mathbf{E}$.

(i)
$$ \mathbf{I}_n \xrightarrow{R_i+cR_j} \mathbf{E} \xrightarrow{R_i-cR_j} \mathbf{I}_n \Rightarrow \mathbf{E} : R_i + cR_j, \mathbf{E}^{-1} : R_i - cR_j. $$

(ii)
$$ \mathbf{I}_n \xrightarrow{R_i \leftrightarrow R_j} \mathbf{E} \xrightarrow{R_i \leftrightarrow R_j} \mathbf{I}_n \Rightarrow \mathbf{E} : R_i \leftrightarrow R_j, \mathbf{E}^{-1} : R_i \leftrightarrow R_j. $$

(iii)
$$ \mathbf{I}_n \xrightarrow{cR_i} \mathbf{E} \xrightarrow{\frac{1}{c}R_i} \mathbf{I}_n \Rightarrow \mathbf{E} : cR_i, \mathbf{E}^{-1} : \frac{1}{c}R_i. $$
